Project Objective
The objective of this project is to provide you with hands-on experience in applying Unsupervised Learning techniques to real-world datasets and in communicating analytical findings effectively to senior stakeholders. You will develop clustering models or dimensionality reduction transformations to identify hidden patterns, group similar observations, or simplify complex datasets. The project emphasizes both analytical rigor and the clear presentation of insights that support data-driven decision-making.

 Final Project Report Requirements 
Dataset Selection and Description: You are required to select a dataset that you are motivated to analyze. The dataset may originate from a public data source, a professional setting, a prior course exercise, or a topic of personal interest. The report must include a dedicated section describing the dataset, summarizing its source, key attributes, and relevance to the chosen analytical objectives.

 Analytical Approach and Model Development:  You must begin by exploring the dataset to understand its structure and quality. This includes performing data cleaning, addressing missing or inconsistent values, and applying feature engineering where necessary.  You must then train at least three variations of the selected unsupervised learning approach, which may involve using different algorithms such as clustering methods or dimensionality reduction transformations, or varying model hyperparameters.  The report should include a clear summary of these model variations and compare their effectiveness in addressing the main objective(s) of the analysis. Optionally, you may revisit a supervised learning model to evaluate whether applying separate models to individual clusters improves performance relative to a single global model.  

Model Selection and Justification: Based on the comparative analysis, you must identify and recommend the unsupervised learning model that best suits the stated objective(s). The report must clearly justify this selection by considering factors such as interpretability, analytical value, and relevance to the dataset and stakeholders.

Key Findings and Insights: The report must include a clearly structured and well-presented section outlining the key findings and insights derived from the selected model. This section should explain how the results support the original objective(s) of the analysis and discuss their practical implications.

Limitations and Future Work: You must critically evaluate the limitations of the analysis, including potential flaws in the data, modeling assumptions, or methodological choices. The report must also propose a detailed plan of action for revisiting the analysis, which may include incorporating additional data, refining features, or applying alternative modeling techniques.

Deliverables and Submission Guidelines
The primary deliverable for this project is a written analytical report submitted as a PDF. The report should focus on insights, findings, and recommendations rather than code implementation. Visualizations may be included where they support the analysis. Submission of code, such as a Python notebook, is optional and may be included as an appendix.

Evaluation Criteria
Your project will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

Inclusion of a clear and comprehensive data description

A well-defined statement of the main objective(s) of the analysis

Comparison of multiple unsupervised learning models with a justified final model selection

Clear presentation of key findings aligned with the project objective(s)
